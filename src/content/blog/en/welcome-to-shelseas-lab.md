Shelsea's Lab is the shelf I keep my public work on. I'm Shelsea Saint-Fleur, a data engineer in Greater Montréal, with a B.Eng. in computer engineering.

Two things are up so far.

**[C’est déjà l’enfer](/projects/cest-deja-lenfer/)** is the live map. It shows road restrictions across Greater Montréal — closed streets, lane removals, worksites, and places where a detour is likely — with driving and walking views. A separate section looks at pothole reports from City of Montréal open data, and it is careful about what those records can and cannot say.

**[Morning Press](/projects/morning-press/)** (*Mon Journal du Matin*) is open source, under the MIT license. It builds a morning newspaper as a PDF, in French or English, from calendars and mail you connect, plus weather, markets, and headlines. You host it yourself. There is no paid product behind it.

This blog is for notes, when there is something worth writing down. The [experience page](/timeline/) is a timeline with the blanks left blank. I would rather show a TODO than invent a job.

The site is bilingual. English lives here. French is under [/fr/](/fr/). It is a static site: no analytics, no cookies, no trackers.
